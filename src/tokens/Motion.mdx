# Motion

Motion in glass-ds should feel like light and depth on a physical pane: quick, soft, and never in the way.
Every value is a token in `src/tokens/tokens.ts`.

<Canvas of={MotionStories.Easings} />

## Which easing for what

| Moment | Easing | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Enter** (dialog, tab indicator, panel, chevron) | `--gds-easing-spring-snappy` | `--gds-duration-spring-snappy` |
| **Exit** (dialog/drawer close, hover-out) | `--gds-easing-out` | `--gds-duration-fast` or `-normal` |
| **Playful confirmation** (press release, switch thumb, radio dot, current page) | `--gds-easing-spring-bounce` | `--gds-duration-spring-bounce` |
| **Large surfaces** (drawer, accordion height, progress fill) | `--gds-easing-spring-gentle` | `--gds-duration-spring-gentle` |
| Colour, background, border | `--gds-easing-standard` | `--gds-duration-fast` |

- A spring easing is always paired with its own duration token: the curve was sampled for that settle time.
- **Fast in, springy out.** Press states (`:active`) switch to `easing-out` + `duration-fast`, so the press
  answers at once; releasing runs the base spring, which settles with a small overshoot.
- **No overshoot on edges or values.** Drawers and the progress bar use the gentle spring, which never passes
  its target: an overshoot would open a gap at the viewport edge or show more progress than there is.
- Exits are shorter than entrances. Nobody waits for something to leave.

## What may animate

- **Only `transform` and `opacity`** (and custom properties feeding them). They run on the compositor.
- Exceptions, all small and isolated: the tab indicator's `width`/`height` (absolutely positioned), the
  accordion's `block-size` (`::details-content` + `interpolate-size`), the checkbox tick's `clip-path`, and
  focus rings (`outline-width`/`outline-offset`, paint only).
- **Never `backdrop-filter` or a blur radius.** Fade or move the glass, never re-blur it.
- Don't fade an ancestor of glass: `opacity < 1` makes it the backdrop root, so the glass inside loses its blur
  until the fade ends. Tab panels that contain glass only rise, they don't fade.
- Write `transition-*` longhands. In browsers without `linear()`, a spring token makes the timing list compute
  to `ease` and the transition still runs; inside the `transition` shorthand it would cancel the whole transition.

## Glass light

`GLASS_SHEEN_CLASS` (or `data-sheen`) adds a specular highlight that sweeps across once on hover or keyboard
focus. Button's glass variant and `<Card interactive>` use it. It takes the element's `::after` and sets
`overflow: clip` (the element's own focus ring is not clipped).

## Accessibility

- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`: nothing moves. Presses, lifts, slides and springs become instant; fades and
  colour changes may stay. Loops (spinner, progress, skeleton) become a slow opacity pulse.
- `forced-colors`, `prefers-contrast: more` and `prefers-reduced-transparency`: the sheen and other decorative
  light are removed.
- Motion never carries meaning on its own: state is always in the DOM (`aria-selected`, `checked`, `open`).
